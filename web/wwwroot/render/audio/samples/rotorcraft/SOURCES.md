## AH-1G cabin/rotor bed — UH-1H/T53 public-domain surrogate

`uh1h_t53_ah1g_surrogate_loop.wav` is an **audio-only gameplay surrogate** conditioned from an
airborne cabin interval in DVIDS
[Iraqi Air Force Helicopter Flight, Part 2](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/71253/iraqi-air-force-helicopter-flight-part-2).
The source aircraft is a UH-1H with T53-family propulsion. It is not an AH-1G recording and the
shipped loop makes no exact AH-1G, BHC-540 rotor, or installation-specific acoustic claim.
