## Buried tube (closed geometry, fiction siting)

Crater-proof dispersed basing: a cut-and-cover launch tube below grade, exiting through a portal into
an open cutting, rather than a hilltop ramp. This removes dependence on terrain that a real theatre
(Ukraine's flat-to-rolling steppe) does not provide — full derivation in
`docs/2026-07-26-buried-launch-tube-and-the-ukraine-theatre.md`.

| Quantity | Value | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gallery bore | 14 × 8 m internal | closed |
| Span vs bore | 7.35 m span ⇒ ~2.7% blockage | closed (see [10 — Geometry](10-geometry.md)) |
| Roofed section | flat run only (433.86 m); 86.14 m ramp arc ridden in the open | closed |
| Berm crest | +10 m | closed |
| Slab datum | 192.0 m MSL; ≥1.0875 m above the 32 m atlas survey under bounded earthworks | closed fiction siting |

**Bore size beats vacuum:** the aircraft's frontal area is a small fraction of the bore, so the tube
behaves like free air with generous vents rather than a pneumatic cannon or an evacuated structure.
Geometry must not grow the aircraft's span without revisiting this bore.

## Theatre siting (fiction)

Ukraine corridor theatre; no maritime presentation. The launch complex sits deep behind the front —
at the aircraft's fuel-fraction-derived radius of action, per
`docs/2026-07-26-buried-launch-tube-and-the-ukraine-theatre.md` §6. Front lines, unit placements, and
specific installation locations are **fiction**: real terrain at real coordinates, invented military
geography on top of it, per the project's fact/fiction discipline.
