## Buried tube (closed interface geometry, fiction siting)

Buried dispersed basing: a cut-and-cover launch tube below grade, exiting through a portal into an
open cutting, rather than a hilltop ramp. This removes dependence on terrain that a real theatre
(Ukraine's flat-to-rolling steppe) does not provide. Burial can reduce exposure; it is **not
crater-proof**. The durable conceptual civil/machinery/cost and generated-presentation basis is
[82 — Launch-gallery engineering basis](82-launch-gallery-engineering-basis.md). The dated
derivation is retained at `docs/2026-07-26-buried-launch-tube-and-the-ukraine-theatre.md`, with its
superseded numerical study labelled.

| Quantity | Value | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gallery bore | 14 × 8 m internal | closed clearance interface; structural section conceptual |
| Span vs bore | 7.35 m span ⇒ ~2.7% nominal blockage | closed span/bore; derived screening value (see [10 — Geometry](10-geometry.md)) |
| Roofed section | flat run only (433.86 m); 86.14 m ramp arc ridden in the open | closed live/presentation interface |
| Berm crest | about +10–11 m | conceptual grading/presentation basis |
| Slab datum | 192.0 m MSL; ≥1.0875 m above the 32 m atlas survey under bounded earthworks | closed fiction siting |

**Bore size beats vacuum:** the aircraft's frontal area is a small fraction of the bore, so the tube
behaves like free air with generous vents rather than a pneumatic cannon or an evacuated structure.
Geometry must not grow the aircraft's span without revisiting this bore.

## Theatre siting (fiction)

Ukraine corridor theatre; no maritime presentation. The launch complex sits deep behind the front —
at the aircraft's fuel-fraction-derived radius of action, per
`docs/2026-07-26-buried-launch-tube-and-the-ukraine-theatre.md` §6. Front lines, unit placements, and
specific installation locations are **fiction**: real terrain at real coordinates, invented military
geography on top of it, per the project's fact/fiction discipline.
