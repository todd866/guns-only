## Doctrine (binding constraints)

1. **AI-first redesign.** ANCA2040 trains for a battlespace where machines hold much of the
   shared mental model and speak much of the R/T. The panel and the radio are that redesign
   made playable: automation may act and narrate, while the human retains the priority scan
   and judgment. See
   `audio/radio/mission/README.md`.
2. **NOW, NEXT, WHO, VERIFY.** ANCA is a four-layer prioritization structure, not an
   automation inventory and not four system-status buckets. It answers what needs attention
   now, what is coming next, who needs to know, and what must be administered or verified.
   Automation ownership is supporting context where it changes those answers.
3. **Presentation must not outrun the kernel.** Every rendered fact binds to sim truth
   already in (or added to) the snapshot. No client-side guessing.
4. **The HUD stays instrument-true.** The panel is a DOM element outside the HUD canvas,
   not a HUD symbol.
5. **Optional means stowed.** Every tier gets one quiet ANCA control, closed by default.
   Opening it is a deliberate cross-check, not a prerequisite for flying the sortie.
6. **One condition, one primary owner.** ANCA may restate the operational meaning of
   instrument truth inside the deliberately opened drawer, but it does not reproduce raw
   instrumentation or create a second unsolicited alarm. A future ANCA-exclusive condition
   may notify only by opting into that behavior explicitly.

## The four layers (binding)

ANCA is ordered because it is a scan, but it is not a contest in which one channel wins and
the other three become irrelevant:

| Layer | Question | Common aviation expression | Examples |
|-------|----------|----------------------------|----------|
| **Aviate — NOW** | What constrains how I can continue flying right now? | Vertical/energy profile, terrain, flight control, limiting fuel | `BINGO 6 MIN`; `AUTO · CLIMB · M0.90 · FL560` |
| **Navigate — NEXT** | What geometry, route, gate, or destination comes next? | Lateral profile | `CVA-31 · 247° · 84 NM`; `MISSION TRACK · 031° · 154 NM` |
| **Communicate — WHO** | Who needs the shared state, and what is the current coordination or airspace posture? | Package, controller, tower, approach, deliberate emissions or silence | `PACKAGE · ENGAGING`; `TOWER · DOWNWIND` |
| **Administrate — VERIFY** | What must be checked, configured, confirmed, or closed out? | Checks and procedural verification | blank during a fight; `RECOVERY · VERIFY 0/2 → GEAR DN LOCKED` |

Vertical/lateral/airspace/checks is the common flight expression, not a hard type system.
Fuel belongs to Aviate when it limits continued flight; Navigate owns the vector to home, not
the fuel calculation. Communicate is a standing coordination posture as well as a momentary
transmission. Administrate is allowed to be blank until a mission transition genuinely creates
verification work.

Each layer publishes at most one current answer. A blank layer is honest and preferred to
filler. Copy states operational meaning rather than copying an instrument: `BINGO 6 MIN`, not
a fuel quantity table; `PACKAGE · ENGAGING`, not a radio transcript.

## Disclosure policy (binding)

A proposed ANCA fact must pass all five gates:

1. **Truth:** the fact binds directly to current snapshot/kernel state.
2. **Layer:** it answers NOW, NEXT, WHO, or VERIFY without forcing a fact into the wrong
   channel. In particular, limiting fuel is not Navigate.
3. **Priority:** it is the one most relevant current answer within that layer: a live
   constraint, phase profile, next geometry, coordination posture, or required verification.
4. **Ownership:** it adds operational meaning instead of reproducing the HUD, systems panel,
   navigation console, limits panel, radio transcript, or action-console alarm. Exception
   detail may be available in the open drawer, but must not create a second unsolicited alarm.
5. **Expiry:** the rule states exactly when the fact disappears. Completed checklists,
   superseded profiles, irrelevant vectors, and pre-limiting fuel arithmetic do not linger.

Tone is semantic, not decorative:

- `active` (blue): a profile, transmission, configuration, or verification is actively moving.
- `steady`: a phase-relevant state the player deliberately asked to cross-check.
- `attention` (amber): an exception visible inside the open drawer.
- `notify`: separate from tone and false by default; only an ANCA-exclusive condition may
  light the stowed control.

## The ANCA panel — UX

Present at every tier, stowable and view-only.

**Stowed (default on portrait and laptop):** one compact `ANCA` control on the right rail,
positioned clear of action consoles and touch reservations. It stays quiet for standing
priorities and for warnings already owned by another surface.

**Open (on request):** a compact four-layer drawer, top-right and outside the HUD zone. The
same interaction and information hierarchy holds on portrait and laptop; portrait keeps
the drawer above the touch controls. Its secondary title is `NOW · NEXT · WHO · VERIFY`.
Only rows that pass the disclosure policy render; if none do, the drawer says
`NO CURRENT PRIORITY IN THESE LAYERS`. Tapping the ANCA
control again stows it immediately.

| Row | Shows when | Example | Supporting truth |
|-----|------------|---------|------------------|
| **A** Aviate | A flight/energy profile is mission-relevant, fuel limits continuation, or a flight constraint requires attention | `BINGO 6 MIN`; `AUTO · CLIMB · M0.90 · FL560` | fuel, mission director, flight systems |
| **N** Navigate | A next vector, gate, route, or destination is known and relevant | `CVA-31 · 247° · 84 NM`; `RAIDER 3 · 090° · 1.0 NM` | navigation and mission geometry |
| **C** Communicate | A coordination/airspace posture exists, including between sparse transmissions | `PACKAGE · ENGAGING`; `TOWER · DOWNWIND` | mission phase plus radio state |
| **A** Administrate | A check, configuration transition, or verification is genuinely due | `LAUNCH · VERIFY 2/4 → GEAR UP` | checklist/configuration truth |

### Current mission expressions

**Guns Only:** Aviate is minutes to bingo once the engagement makes fuel limiting;
Navigate is the vector to home plate; Communicate is `PACKAGE · ENGAGING` until the
handoff/RTB transition makes it `PACKAGE · EGRESSING`; Administrate is blank because the
current sim does not yet publish a truthful post-fight weapons-safe verification.

**Rapier Intercept/Circuits:** Aviate is the authored Mach/altitude or KTAS/pattern profile,
with `AUTO`, `PILOT`, `DEMO`, `DIRECT`, or `MONITOR` as ownership context; Navigate is the
mission vector, pattern leg, or home vector; Communicate is the controller/tower/approach
posture; Administrate is the active launch/commit/RTB/recovery verification.

**Low-Level Drone:** Aviate may expose limiting fuel; Navigate owns the active raider vector;
Communicate carries the defensive-intercept posture. An ammunition-per-target administrative
rule remains future work until its threshold and expiry are authored rather than guessed.

**Medevac:** the dedicated mission presentation remains the owner. The same layers inform its
design—flight/masking now, pickup/receiver next, crew/ground/receiver coordination, custody and
handoff verification—but ANCA does not duplicate that surface.

## Checklist engine (the one new sim subsystem)

`sim/MissionChecklists.cs` — checklist definitions as **data** per beat phase. V1
checklists, constrained to items with real sim truth behind them (implementation research
2026-07-29): **LAUNCH** (cat stroke, airborne, gear up, flaps up), **COMMIT** (the
fence-in substitute — Intercept-phase commit + weapons authorized; the sim has no
master-arm/fence switch), **RECOVERY** (gear down and locked, flaps landing — no hook
state exists [the hook is fixed geometry] and no harness state exists, so those items are
cut rather than faked). Design:

- Every item is a **predicate over sim truth** evaluated at tick boundaries. V1 verification
  completes automatically because those are the actions currently modeled. That implementation
  fact does not define the Administrate layer; future player or crew verification may appear
  only after the sim has authoritative state for the action and its completion.
- Completion events are exposed to `MissionRadio` so admin milestones can be narrated
  in-register ("fence in" call) — the C and A channels tie together.
- State resets on beat start; each predicate fires exactly once per checklist activation.

**Snapshot additions:** a small `checklist_*` block — id/name, done count, total,
next-pending item label. Full contract ritual: `SnapshotHotFrame` LayoutVersion 16 → 17,
schema 1.21.0 → 1.22.0 in `SnapshotProjection.cs` **and both** `pack.json` copies, plus
every test pin (including the escaped-regex pins in
`g_tolerance_bridge_contract.test.mjs`).

## Modules and data flow

New `web/wwwroot/render/anca/`:

- `anca_view_model.js` — pure function: snapshot → four layer structs
  (`{letter, key, line, tone, shown, notify}`) plus the filtered `shownRows`. A/N/C
  compose from current mission fields; the fourth row binds verification/configuration truth.
  The medical sim can later re-skin the first channel as Attention without touching the renderer.
- `anca_panel.js` — DOM renderer (stowed control + optional drawer), wired in `app.js`; styles
  follow existing panel CSS conventions.

## Error handling

- Missing, complete, superseded, or irrelevant fields → the row is omitted; never fabricate
  around missing truth.
- No rows passing the disclosure policy → one bounded empty-state line.
- Missing snapshot, ready screen, finished screen, and the dedicated casevac presentation
  hide and re-stow the drawer.
