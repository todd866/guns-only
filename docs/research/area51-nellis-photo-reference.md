## 1. Nellis Base Exchange (BX) — real interior photos FOUND
All 12 are Commons `File:Nellis AFB Exchange (<id>).jpg`, **CC BY 2.0** (US Air Force Flickr, Nov 2015 grand re-opening of the 221,800 sq ft remodel). Page: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nellis_AFB_Exchange_(ID).jpg`. Local `bx-interior-NN`:

Official PD set (reference-only link, 403 to script): `https://www.nellis.af.mil/News/Photos/igphoto/2001344817` and `/2001344818` (ribbon cutting); article `https://www.nellis.af.mil/News/Article/664695/nafb-exchange-holds-grand-opening-ceremony`; DVIDS video "Nellis Main Exchange Grand Re-Opening", 10 Nov 2015.

Modelable facts (from the photos I viewed: 01, 03, 05, 06, 08, 10, 11, 12):
- **Ceiling**: suspended 2x2 ft acoustic tile grid, off-white (#E8E4D6), ~4 m high. Recessed 2x4 fluorescent troffers in long regular rows (very repetitive; rows read as a "runway" down each aisle). No exposed trusses.
- **Floor**: two zones. Main concourse/food-court/entrance = **polished sealed concrete**, glossy, warm amber-tan (#B09A6E) with strong reflections and saw-cut joints. Soft-goods and shoe departments = **honey wood-look laminate** (#C99A5B).
- **Walls**: pale butter-yellow paint (#EDE6A8) above the fixtures; accent walls in olive-mustard (#A89A2E), orange slat-wall (#D2693A), dark wood veneer panels (#6B3A22) at the food court. Cream square columns (#E4DDA0) with grey metal base trim.
- **Fixtures**: black (#1E1E1E) gondola shelving with orange-yellow (#E9A820) header bands carrying white caps department names ("WOMEN'S BLOUSES"); chrome racks; shallow-radius "island" tables with stainless tops on pale-maple (#D8B98A) wedge bases (electronics).
- **Signage**: free-standing hanging signs on poles (Power Zone: black panel, white+yellow lettering, photo graphic), a central square drop-soffit light well over the concourse with a lantern pendant; green "grow"-style kids' header; red CLEARANCE and green SALE hanging cards.
- **Food court**: opens off the main concourse to the left of the entrance atrium; lowered soffit with a dark navy "FOOD COURT" sign; chain counters with mosaic-tile backsplash (maroon/olive/gold, #7A2E3A/#8A8A2E) over blond-wood counters (#D9A87A).
- **Entrance**: glass storefront with anti-theft pedestals (white/grey towers); large wall mural ("Our Customers Are Heroes", jets, night Strip skyline, F-22) is the arrival feature. Carts: grey wire, blue handles.
- No exterior shots found (the "exchange" Commons hits are all interior). Exterior must be built from the 2015 article text: new storefront, mural + Wall of Honor outside the main store, outdoor living area beside Power Zone.

**Commissary**: `nellis-commissary-01` = Commons "'Frozen Food Month' brings commissary savings 150223-A-ZW947-001" (**PD**, Kevin Robinson). Facts: white 2x4 drop ceiling with long tubular fixtures, glossy pale-grey/cream VCT floor (#C8C4B8), black glass-door freezer wall (left) with navy header board and white snowflakes, black island freezers, navy-blue promo banners. Shoppette: not found.

## 2. Nellis base housing
No ground-level photo found. Aerials (`nellis-flickr-03`/`04`, Commons "Nellis Air Force Base, Las Vegas, Nevada (14017031619 / 7981492388)", **CC BY-SA 2.0**) show dense curvilinear cul-de-sac subdivisions west/south of the flightline, rows of **terracotta/red tile roofs (#B5593C)** on tan stucco (#D8C3A0), tiny lots, a park with a ball field. Official text (housing.af.mil welcome packet, 2026): 5 communities, ~1,100 homes, "contemporary and traditional Mediterranean and Mission style", covered front porches, fenced rear patios, community centers and pools.
- Low-pitch concrete-tile hip roofs; stucco in sand/tan/cream; garage-forward fronts.
- Yards = gravel xeriscape (#B59E78) with a few palms/desert shrubs, rock borders; block/stucco privacy walls (#CDB892).
- Streets: wide, pale asphalt (#6E6E70), rolled curbs, mailbox clusters, desert-tan street signs.

- `strip-aerial-02` Commons "Las Vegas Strip Aerial September 2013" (CC BY-SA 4.0), view from SOUTH, so mirror in your head: from north the **STRAT tower (needle + saucer)** is the nearest silhouette, then Wynn/Encore bronze slabs, Venetian/Palazzo, then the Mandalay gold block far end.

Scale rules: ~2 m aisles, 1.2 m shelves, 30 m gondola runs, 4 m ceilings, rows of ceiling lights as a built-in speed cue.

## Brand marks to avoid
Exchange/AAFES star-and-text logo, "Power Zone", "BE FIT", "Our Customers Are Heroes", real department-store tenants (Lauren/Ralph Lauren, Carter's, Asics, Under Armour, Nike, Apple, Sony, Samsung, Starbucks), Commissary logo and green bag, USAF "Hap Arnold wings" symbol, Thunderbirds marks/paint, "Home of the Fighter Pilot" slogan verbatim, Nellis/99 ABW crests, STRAT and casino names, the Welcome sign design. Use invented equivalents ("Zone Tech", "Cadet Cafe", "Desert Mart").
