# F-22 visual-merge slice

Audit: `docs/grok-overhaul/audit/f22.md` section 5, items 1–4. Item 5 (pitch assist and first-pass hold-fire) was skipped on purpose.

The front door, the Ace opening, and the first-person view are unchanged. The Kestrel valley keeps the old southwest runway so this move does not retarget that sortie.

## 1. Finite sortie, landing is the ending

The visual-merge beat now bills two engagements, the same cap Top Gun already uses. Splashing that cap and making a survivable full stop is `SortieOutcome.Victory`. Knock-it-off or Bingo while a billed fight is still left stays `Discontinued`.

The cap is complete only when the last engagement is actually splashed and nobody has already called it off. After that splash, return-to-base is available with the sky empty, which is the same hole Top Gun and the valley already special-cased.

Tests: `F22FiniteSortieTests.RecoveredStopAfterTheCapIsVictory`, `F22FiniteSortieTests.BingoBeforeTheCapRecoversAsDiscontinued`.

No debrief frame. The snap helper flies 14 seconds of the merge. It does not fly the pattern or the full stop. The debrief copy was left alone: a recovered handoff is still "Handoff Complete · Home", not a letter grade.

## 2. Runway inside the streamed world

The home slab moved from about 45 NM southwest to a 015° strip whose threshold is `(600 m, 77.50 m, 19 400 m)`, about 13 NM north of the merge and inside the 48 km stream. Both ends stay inside that disc.

Survey, from the same atlas the kernel loads, sampled every 25 m under the 3 000 × 45 m pavement: terrain 74.230–77.178 m. The 77.50 m slab is 0.32–3.27 m of fill. About 98 m of relief sits 2.5 km off the centreline. Provisional site. Atlas-measured heights. Not a real airfield and not an F-22 landing distance.

Tests: `F22FiniteSortieTests.NorthRunwayIsInsideTheStreamingRadius`, `UkraineTerrainTruthTests.F22RecoveryRunwayStillFollowsTheAtlasTerrainDatum`, `FuelModelTests.ModernVisualMergeAuthorsAHomeAndReserveAboveMinimumFuel`.

Headless merge frame: `.grok-shots/f22-flight.png` (port 8940, ANGLE Metal, Apple M5). Terrain relief is in the picture. The pavement is not a readable mark from 10 000 ft at 13 NM, nearly end-on. The 14-second helper does not fly down to it.

## 3. Overhead, one call per leg

The runway pattern is initial on the extended centreline at 1 500 ft, break at the numbers, perch 1 NM abeam at 1 000 ft, then the existing 3° final. Capturing initial advances to the break and does not skip it. A missed final still recycles to initial. Altitudes are a stable surrogate for the overhead described in AFI 11-202 Volume 3, not an F-22 local supplement.

Radio, one pilot call when the gate changes, and not on the ingress chevrons: "Ghost One One, initial." / "breaking." / "gear down." / "final." The FAST / ON SPEED / SLOW chip is unchanged.

Tests: `ConventionalRunwayPatternRecoveryTests.ScheduleIsANamedLeftHandRunwayPatternNotADirectIngress`, `EntryCaptureRequiresAltitudeAndHeadingBeforeGuidanceAdvances`, `F22FiniteSortieTests.OverheadSpeaksOncePerLegAndInitialDoesNotSkipTheBreak`, `MissionRadioCatalogContractTests` (the four lines are in `audio/radio/mission/lines.json`).

No downwind-chevron frame. The helper never enters the pattern.

## 4. 480-round magazine

`CombatConfig.ModernVisualMerge` sets `PlayerInfiniteAmmo: false`. The session already builds a finite `GunKill` from that flag, and the HUD already prints `ammo`. Firing past 480 does not produce another round. A short burst leaves `480 - rounds fired`. Winchester was already the radio call when the magazine hits zero. It does not auto-RTB. That call is the decision, next to Bingo.

Test: `F22FiniteSortieTests.MagazineStopsAtFourHundredEightyRounds`.

## Review fixes

The overhead is a reciprocal downwind and a descending 180, not a perch aimed at final. Initial stays on the extended centreline at 1 500 ft and 300 KCAS. The break is over the numbers. Downwind is 1 NM left of the landing heading, flown back to a perch abeam the touchdown aim point, and that perch is the gear-down gate. Three approach gates (45°, 90°, 135°) carry a continuous left turn onto a short final 1 500 m from the threshold, still on the physical 3° line. A missed final still recycles to initial. Capture heading is 30°, so a cut from the perch straight at final does not advance the leg.

These numbers are a surrogate for the overhead in AFI 11-202 Volume 3, not an F-22 supplement and not a NATOPS. The same director still serves every F-22 conventional recovery, including Ace Duel, the Kestrel first run, and practice. They were all on this schedule; the six-gate perch-to-final path was the defect, so the geometry was corrected in place rather than split.

"Gear down" is transmitted only when `GearDownAndLocked` is actually set. A clean perch does not latch the call, so lowering the gear on that same gate still speaks. `OverheadDoesNotCallGearDownWhileTheGearIsUp`. The pattern assertions are in `ScheduleIsANamedLeftHandRunwayPatternNotADirectIngress` and `MissedFinalBeyondTheAimRestartsAtPatternEntry`.
